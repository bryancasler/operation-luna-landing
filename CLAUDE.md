## Stopping and continuing

When a step doesn't need my input, keep going. Put status notes in the same message as your next action.
Stop and ask only when you can't continue without me, or before anything destructive or visible outside this repository: deleting data or branches, force-pushing, rewriting history, deploying, or posting anywhere other people will see it.
In this repo, pushing to `main` is deploying: GitHub Pages publishes the live site from it.
When I describe a problem or ask a question, the deliverable is your assessment: report findings and stop.
